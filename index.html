About Me

Hi, I'm Sophia, nice to meet you!

I'm from Indonesia, Southeast Asia. I'm the second of three siblings and the only daughter in my family (yes, I'm used to being outnumbered 😄).

I graduated in Manufacturing Automation Engineering and Mechatronics, but beyond my technical background, I'm someone who loves creativity, crafting, volunteering, cooking, and spending meaningful time with children.

I enjoy making things with my hands, exploring new ideas, learning from different people, and finding happiness in the little things. I believe life gets more beautiful when we keep learning, sharing, and creating memories with others.

Why Au Pair?

Why I Chose This Journey.

Because I want to see the world with my own eyes, not just through pictures. One of my biggest dreams is to travel around the world, experience different seasons, and finally feel the snow in my hands ❄️ (I've never touched real snow. Please be patient with my excitement!)

I'm curious about everything new: cultures, languages, traditions, food, and ways of living. I love learning, and I believe living with a host family is one of the most meaningful ways to understand another part of the world.

But there's another reason: I genuinely enjoy being around children. Playing with them, helping them discover new things, and watching their little personalities grow makes me really happy.

I also have a personal goal: by the age of 30, I hope to have visited at least 10 countries. Becoming an Au Pair is one of the first meaningful steps I'm ready to take toward it.

Some dreams may seem small to others, but to me, every new country is a whole new world to discover.

A Day in My Life.

My Experience with Children

I enjoy spending time with children, and I believe every child deserves to feel supported, understood, and encouraged.

Through volunteering, I once got to be a big sister figure to a child at a volunteer event. We did a painting activity together, and our work even won best painting! 🎨

But honestly, winning wasn't the best part. What mattered was helping that child discover their creativity, see their own potential, and feel confident expressing themselves.

I love encouraging children to explore their talents and take little steps toward their own achievements.

A Proud Aunt of Three

I'm also an aunt to three wonderful nieces and nephews!

I'm actively involved in their everyday lives: helping them eat, playing, learning new things, keeping them company, and yes, handling tantrums and understanding big emotions too.

Being around little ones is a familiar part of my life. Every child has a different personality, different needs, and different ways of expressing themselves.

I don't expect every child to behave the same way. I believe patience, understanding, and consistency matter most.

A Creative Playmate

Crafting is one of my favorite hobbies! I love making handmade flowers, little decorations, and creative projects.

I'd love to bring that creativity into everyday activities with children: drawing, painting, paper crafts, storytelling, and anything that sparks imagination.

Learning doesn't always have to happen through books. Sometimes the best lessons come from playing and creating together.

A Helping Hand at Home

I'm happy to help with light household tasks in everyday family life, such as:

  • Washing dishes.
  • Folding and organizing laundry.
  • Putting clean clothes away.
  • Cleaning the dining table after meals.
  • Tidying up toys and personal belongings.
  • Keeping shared spaces neat and organized.
  • Returning household items to their proper places.

I value cleanliness and organization, and I believe a comfortable home is something everyone contributes to.

Why should you choose me?

A Heart Full of Curiosity, A Mind Ready to Learn.

I know being an Au Pair is more than traveling abroad. It means taking responsibility, adapting to a new environment, respecting another family's lifestyle, and being someone they can trust.

My dream of exploring the world gives me the courage to take this big step, but I don't want to chase it unprepared. I've spent time building my skills, gaining experience with children, and becoming more independent.

I don't smoke, vape, or use drugs. I value a healthy lifestyle and want to stay active enough to enjoy all the adventures ahead (and to keep up with energetic kids!).

I'm not looking for a shortcut to see the world. I'm looking for a meaningful experience where I can contribute, learn, and grow.

If I join your family, I promise to:

  • Respect your family rules, values, and cultural traditions.
  • Be responsible, trustworthy, and respectful.
  • Communicate openly and honestly.
  • Take my childcare responsibilities seriously.
  • Be willing to learn and adapt.
  • Respect your home and contribute positively to everyday family life.
I want to travel far, but I also want to leave kindness wherever I go.

My dream host family

A Home Away From Home.

I hope to find a host family who welcomes me not only as an Au Pair, but as a meaningful part of everyday life. I'd love a home where we share stories, learn from each other, explore cultures, and make beautiful memories together.

A loving and welcoming family

I hope to find a family who treats me with kindness, respect, and openness, just as I'll treat them.

Children with different needs are welcome

I'm open to caring for children with special needs or disabilities. Every child is unique and deserves love, patience, and room to grow. I'm happy to learn about their individual needs and follow your guidance.

Single mothers are welcome

I'm open to joining a single-mother household. I'm comfortable with that family arrangement and would be happy to be a supportive part of it.

Languages and culture

I'd love to join an English-speaking household so we can communicate comfortably. If your family speaks another language at home, I'd be genuinely excited to learn it! A new language is a beautiful way to understand a culture.
